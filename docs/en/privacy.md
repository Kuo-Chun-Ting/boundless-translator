## Data collection

Boundless Translator does not require an account and does not use a developer-operated server. It contains no advertising, tracking, analytics, or third-party crash reporting. The developer does not collect selected text, screenshots, translations, clipboard contents, preferences, or subscription status.

## Handling translation content

The app uses macOS Accessibility to read selected text. If needed, it simulates Command-C and reads the clipboard. Before copying, it temporarily saves the existing clipboard contents in memory and restores them after reading the selected text, provided no later clipboard update has been observed. Other copy operations happening at the same time or later can still affect restoration.

Screenshot translation uses the macOS region-capture tool and on-device Apple Vision text recognition. The app deletes the temporary screenshot after capture, keeps no screenshot or translation history, and does not send this content to the developer.

Translation, speech, and Lookup use Apple system features and services. Apple handles its services under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Local data

The app stores language and shortcut preferences on your Mac. They remain until you change them or remove the app’s local data; deleting the app alone may leave these settings on your Mac.

- To request instructions for clearing local settings and dismissed tips, email [halila@me.com](mailto:halila@me.com).
  - Specify whether you use the Mac App Store, TestFlight, or DMG Test edition.
  - Clearing local data does not cancel your Apple subscription or reset macOS permissions.

## Subscriptions and payments

The Mac App Store edition uses Apple StoreKit for purchases, renewals, restoration, and subscription management. Apple processes account and payment information. The app reads verified subscription status only to determine feature access. The developer does not receive payment details.

## Permissions

- **Accessibility:** reads selected text and simulates Command-C to copy it when needed.
- **Screen Recording:** captures a region for screenshot translation.

You can change these permissions in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Disabling a permission prevents the related feature from working.
