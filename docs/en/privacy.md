## Data handling

Boundless Translator does not require an account and does not use a developer-operated server. It contains no advertising, tracking, analytics, or third-party crash reporting. The developer does not collect selected text, screenshots, translations, clipboard contents, preferences, or subscription status.

## Translation and screenshots

The app uses macOS Accessibility to read selected text. If needed, it simulates Command-C and reads the clipboard. This fallback may replace the previous clipboard contents.

Screenshot translation uses the macOS region-capture tool and VisionKit Live Text. The app deletes the temporary screenshot after capture, keeps no screenshot or translation history, and does not send this content to the developer.

Translation, speech, and Lookup use Apple system frameworks and services. Apple handles its services under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Local settings and subscriptions

The app stores language and shortcut preferences on your Mac.

The Mac App Store edition uses Apple StoreKit for purchases, renewals, restoration, and subscription management. Apple processes account and payment information. The app reads verified subscription status only to determine feature access. The developer does not receive payment details.

## Permissions

- **Accessibility:** reads selected text and supports the Command-C fallback.
- **Screen Recording:** captures a region for screenshot translation.

You can change these permissions in System Settings > Privacy & Security. Disabling a permission prevents the related feature from working.

## Contact and changes

Questions about privacy can be sent to [halila@me.com](mailto:halila@me.com). This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change.
