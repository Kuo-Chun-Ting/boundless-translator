# Boundless Translator

Translate text across apps on your Mac with a simple keyboard shortcut.

## Why we built it

Translation is often tied to a browser, website, or a specific app. When you move to another app, the same translation tool may no longer be available. Boundless Translator gives you one simple way to translate while you read, wherever you are working on your Mac.

## Translate selected text

Select text in another app and press your translation shortcut. The original text and translation appear side by side, so you can keep reading without copying text into a translation website.

## Translate text you cannot select

Capture the area you want to translate with the screenshot shortcut. In the captured image, select the text and press your translation shortcut. This works for text in images and app interfaces that you cannot select directly.

## Look up words

Select a word or phrase in the original text to look it up in the dictionary. You can also play the original text or translation aloud.

## Try it in your favorite app

Choose a sentence you want to understand, select it, and press your translation shortcut. Keep reading right where you are.
